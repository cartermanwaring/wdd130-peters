Gear & Sky Prep

Everything you need before heading outside

Before You Head Out

  • Check the weather forcast for clear skies and low cloud cover
  • Pick a spot away from bright city lights if you can

Simple Essentials

  • Dress in warm layers (nights cool down fast)
  • Use a dim or red light to protect your night vision
  • Bring a sky map app or planisphere to find your bearings
